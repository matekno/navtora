# Photos of real sifrei

Columns read by NavTorah, shown in the blog post on how its network was trained. `<id>.webp` is the photo, scaled down, and `<id>-analysis.webp` is the same photo with what the app found drawn over it, made by `tools/eval/src/blog-real.ts`.

- **shannon**: page 1 of [Sefer-Torah-Elihu-Shannon-2a.pdf](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sefer-Torah-Elihu-Shannon-2a.pdf), by the scribe Elihu Shannon, Kibbutz Sa'ad, 2011. CC BY-SA 3.0. Both images here, with and without the drawing, are CC BY-SA 3.0 too.
- **kokhav**: page 1 of [David-kokhav-sefer-torah.pdf](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:David-kokhav-sefer-torah.pdf), by Rabbi David Kokhav. CC BY-SA 3.0. Both images here are CC BY-SA 3.0 too.
- **makhonot**: image n10 of [Torah Scroll (Germany ca. 1920s, refurbished Makhon Ot no. 18157)](https://archive.org/details/torah-scroll-germany-ca.-1920s-refurbished-makhon-ot-no.-18157), Internet Archive. CC0.
- **bl-sheet**, **bl1** to **bl7**: image n10 of [British Library MS Or. 1462](https://archive.org/details/sefer-torah-bl-or-1462-images), 15th century, Internet Archive. Public domain. bl1 to bl7 are its columns, right to left, cut with `tools/eval/src/crop-columns.ts`.
